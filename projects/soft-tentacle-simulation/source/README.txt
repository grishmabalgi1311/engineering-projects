Single-fin pixel tank - new recreation, not recovered old code.

Launch START_SINGLE_FIN.cmd. Starts manual with no automatic movement.
Double-click a tentacle segment, then Ctrl + right-drag to apply force.
C/U: clear tendon forces and demo. B: reset all. Space: pause.
M: optional tendon oscillation. I/K and O/L: tendon forces.
Blue cells show surface elevation (color contrast enhanced, height unchanged).
Light square tracers advect with simulated flow, without artificial velocity scaling.
Tank: 0.55 x 0.32 m. Water depth: 0.035 m.
This is an uncalibrated reduced model, not validated water physics.
The ball responds to local water drag and surface slope; no target attraction.
