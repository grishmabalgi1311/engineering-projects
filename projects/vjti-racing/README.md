## Setup and verification boundary

Original side, top and perspective CAD views are included. They document a student-team competition vehicle. The full ATV CAD assembly was not located in the inspected files. The portfolio workbench is a simplified, normalized reconstruction from these views; it is not the manufacturing CAD or a vehicle-dynamics validation. Subsystem dimensions, reductions in mass, and measured durability improvements are not invented.
